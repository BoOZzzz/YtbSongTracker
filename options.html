    What This Extension Does

    Ytb Song Tracker helps you build a personal listening history from YouTube and YouTube Music, then turns that history into stats you can actually browse.

    Purpose

    It is designed to answer simple personal questions like what songs you played most this month, what videos were misclassified, and how your listening changed over time.

    What Data Is Stored

    The extension stores local listen records such as title, identified artist when available, source type, listened time, playback date, and classification result.

    Where It Lives

    Your listen history and stats are stored locally in the extension. An optional backend can help with Spotify lookup caching and metadata enrichment, but it is not the primary source of truth for your personal history.

    How Songs Are Counted

    A listen is counted once playback crosses your configured threshold. After that, the same playback session can continue updating the listened time while you keep watching or listening.

    Current dashboard sections

    • Monthly top songs
    • Expanded song rankings beyond the top 30
    • Full history for non-song items
    • Manual correction tools for song/video classification
    • Imported listened-history support for JSON files such as Spotify exports

    Settings

    Configure thresholds and optional metadata-backend behavior while the product is still in development.

    Import History

    Bring your Spotify listening history into the tracker by downloading your export as JSON, then importing it here.

    1. Request Your Spotify Data

    Open Spotify privacy settings and request your account data export. Spotify will email you a downloadable archive when it is ready.

    Open Spotify privacy settings

    2. Find the JSON Files

    After downloading the archive, unzip it and look for streaming-history JSON files. The extension supports common Spotify export shapes from both legacy and extended history exports.

    3. Import Into The Extension

    Choose one JSON file below and import it. You can repeat this for multiple Spotify files if your export includes more than one history file.

    Supported Spotify Fields

    Legacy exports often include endTime, artistName, trackName, and msPlayed. Extended exports often include ts, master_metadata_track_name, master_metadata_album_artist_name, and ms_played.
